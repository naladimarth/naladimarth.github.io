01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Financial Calculators: NalaFin, displayed as NalaFin on your device and published by Naladimarth. It explains the information handled by the Android app and these app-specific legal pages, and the choices available to you.

The same app may appear on Google Play as Finanzrechner: NalaFin in German or Calculadora financiera NalaFin in Spanish. Its Android package is com.naladimarth.sipcalculatornetworth.

Calculations run on your device. The app does not require an account, connect to bank accounts or provide a cloud-sync service for your financial records. Limited technical diagnostics and communications you choose to send are described separately below. The operator and privacy contact are identified at the bottom of this page.

02

Information on your device

Depending on the features you use, NalaFin processes and stores:

  • Your optional display name, shared profile date of birth, currency, number format and language preferences.
  • Calculator and planner inputs, dates, assumptions, confirmations, drafts and results, including income, expenses, debts, housing information and goals.
  • Asset and liability labels and amounts, Net Worth totals and targets.
  • Children’s names or nicknames, birthdays and planned goals, plus parent age or birthday information where a selected calculation needs it.
  • Saved calculation names and dates, original inputs and results, historical context and pinned-record preferences.
  • Local disclaimer, introduction and reporting preferences, recovery information, and limited launch-count and review-prompt records.

This information supports calculations, restores preferences and drafts, and displays saved records. It is held in Android app-private storage. We do not operate a remote financial database for it.

The app does not intentionally transmit financial entries, display names, dates of birth or children’s details to us or Firebase. Information you choose to send in an email or publish in a review is separate. Use only details needed for a calculation; do not enter bank passwords or unnecessary identity information.

03

Dates of birth and saved history

The shared profile date of birth supports age-based planning and is required for a new Financial Freedom Planner calculation. You can edit or remove it in Settings. Resetting planner inputs does not necessarily remove that shared value.

Saved results preserve their original context. A saved Freedom calculation can retain the birthday and age used when it was created. Saved children’s plans can retain names, birthdays and goals. Editing or removing a current profile or draft does not rewrite these historical records.

Delete the relevant saved records as well if you want to remove their historical details. Older installations may also retain local migration or recovery copies. The deletion controls and their limits are explained below.

04

Crash reporting and your choice

Crash reporting is ON by default in the release app. Google Firebase Crashlytics helps identify crashes, app-not-responding events and technical failures. Turn it off or on at Settings → Privacy → Crash reporting. Financial calculations remain available when it is off.

There is no separate reporting-consent popup. Reporting may begin before you accept the educational disclaimer. Accepting that disclaimer acknowledges calculation limitations; it is not consent to diagnostic processing.

Diagnostic information

Reports can include crash traces, technical error categories, app version and package details, Android and device information, network type, relevant app state, installation identifiers and limited session information. Firebase Installations and Sessions support installation and quality metrics. Google also processes service-operation information, which can include IP addresses.

We use diagnostics to investigate failures and improve reliability, not to advertise to you or assess your finances. App-generated reports filter original exception messages and restrict technical details. We do not intentionally attach financial records, names, birthdays or contact details. This filtering is not a guarantee that every Android- or SDK-generated diagnostic is anonymous or free of incidental information.

What switching off does

The app disables its reporting and requests deletion of unsent Crashlytics reports. It cannot retract a transfer already in progress or delete reports already received by Google. Re-enabling reporting is designed to discard older pending reports rather than intentionally upload reports retained during an off period. The switch is not a guarantee that all supporting-service network activity immediately stops.

Installation identifiers are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. See Firebase’s privacy information and its Android data disclosures.

05

Support emails and Google Play reviews

Support emails

The app can open an editable draft in your email application containing available app version/build, Android version, device manufacturer/model and app language. You can change or remove these details before sending.

The app does not send email itself or automatically attach financial records, profile details, screenshots or logs. If you send a message, we receive your sender address, message, attachments and any details you include. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle related requests or disputes. Email delivery, forwarding and mailbox providers also process the message.

Please redact private information from screenshots and avoid sending passwords, bank credentials or unnecessary information about children.

Ratings and reviews

Google Play reviews are optional. The app keeps limited local information to manage prompt timing; it does not receive your review text or confirmation that you submitted a rating. No saved financial payload is sent with the review request. Reviews may be public; testing feedback may be visible to the developer. Manage or delete reviews through Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy practices.

06

Permissions, advertising and tracking

The app uses internet and network-state access for diagnostics and connected services such as Google Play review requests. External links can open your browser or email application.

The current app does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, shared files, SMS or call logs. It does not connect to financial accounts or request banking credentials.

The current release contains no advertisements, Google Mobile Ads SDK or advertising-ID permission. It does not use Firebase Analytics, Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database or Cloud Messaging. Diagnostics described above can still be processed when reporting is on.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Local financial records are not used for advertising profiles, credit scoring or automated lending or eligibility decisions.

07

Who can receive information

  • Google/Firebase: technical diagnostics and supporting service information.
  • Email delivery, forwarding and mailbox providers: support messages and attachments you choose to send.
  • GitHub: website hosting and related request/security information.
  • Google Play and external services you open: information associated with your direct use of their services.

The developer handles support and reviews diagnostics for the purposes described here. Information we actually hold may also be disclosed where necessary to meet a binding legal requirement, respond to a lawful request, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims, subject to applicable safeguards. This does not give us remote access to records held only on your device.

08

How long information is kept

Local records

Kept until changed or removed through the app or Android, or lost through device/storage failure. Deleting an individual record removes it from active history; migration or recovery copies from older installations may remain locally until a full reset or Android storage deletion.

Crashlytics information

Google states that crash traces and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Complete deletion on day 90 is not guaranteed. Supporting Firebase service data follows Google’s applicable retention practices. Turning reporting off does not erase already received reports.

Support correspondence

We delete or anonymise support correspondence within 12 months after a case is resolved, except necessary information retained longer for a documented legal obligation or genuine dispute. It is removed when that need ends. Earlier deletion requests are considered under applicable law.

Other providers

Website logs and Google Play information follow the relevant provider’s retention practices. Resetting the app does not delete website logs, support emails or Play reviews.

09

Your controls and deletion

  • Turn reporting off or on in Settings → Privacy → Crash reporting.
  • Edit or remove profile details in Settings.
  • Delete individual saved calculations and Net Worth entries using their controls. Remove historical snapshots separately when needed.
  • Confirm Reset App Data to clear app-managed financial and profile records, related preferences and retained migration copies. It also attempts to turn reporting off; check any error shown and the Privacy switch.
  • Clear app storage through Android or uninstall to remove local app storage.
  • Email support@naladimarth.com about information you have sent us.

The in-app reset does not erase every independent onboarding or SDK record, perform forensic secure erasure, or delete diagnostics already received by Google.

A fresh installation or clearing Android app storage removes the saved reporting preference, so reporting defaults to on again.

There is no NalaFin account to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase financial records stored only on your device. An email alone may not identify a particular diagnostic report because it is not linked to a named NalaFin account. We explain relevant limits rather than promise deletion we cannot perform.

10

Security and backup limits

Local information relies on Android app-private storage and device security. The app does not add a separate database-encryption layer or its own app passcode. Use a secure device lock and take care when sharing your device or screenshots.

The app is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Manufacturer and system behaviour can differ. We do not provide a cloud copy from which we can restore your records.

Firebase uses HTTPS for information in transit. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Keep independent records of important information. We will handle security incidents and provide notices where applicable law requires.

11

Teenagers and children’s information

NalaFin is intended for teenagers and adults and is not designed for young children. The Children’s Future Planner lets a person plan for a child; it does not create a child account or communicate with a child.

Only enter another person’s details when you have appropriate authority. Use only what is needed for the calculation and consider a nickname. Children’s details can remain in drafts and saved snapshots until removed.

Contact us if you believe we have received a child’s information inappropriately. We will assess the concern and take appropriate steps under applicable law. This audience statement does not claim that the app provides age verification or a parental-consent system.

The developer operates from India. Google/Firebase, hosting providers and email providers may process information in other countries where their infrastructure or service providers are located. Local financial records are not transferred to us merely because you use the app outside India.

Cross-border processing is subject to applicable data-protection requirements. Google’s arrangements are described in Firebase’s privacy information. You may contact us about processing locations and safeguards relevant to your information.

This notice explains the app’s current behaviour. A default-on setting, silence, continued use or acceptance of the educational disclaimer is not treated as affirmative privacy consent. This notice does not replace any consent or other safeguards required by applicable law.

Send requests to support@naladimarth.com and explain the information and request concerned. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or authority. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested and necessary.

We respond within applicable legal time limits and explain any lawful reason a request cannot be fulfilled. You may approach a competent authority without first contacting us. Nothing here limits mandatory rights or remedies. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Policy updates

Absturzberichte und Ihre Wahl

Absturzberichte sind in der veröffentlichten App standardmäßig eingeschaltet. Google Firebase Crashlytics hilft, Abstürze, Fälle einer nicht reagierenden App und technische Fehler zu erkennen. Es gibt kein gesondertes Einwilligungsfenster für Absturzberichte. Die Berichterstattung kann beginnen, bevor Sie den Hinweis zur Nutzung für Bildungszwecke akzeptieren. Mit dessen Annahme erkennen Sie die Grenzen der Berechnungen an; sie ist keine Einwilligung in die Verarbeitung von Diagnosedaten.

Diagnoseinformationen

Berichte können Absturz-Stacktraces, technische Fehlerkategorien, App-Version und Paketangaben, Android- und Geräteinformationen, Netzwerktyp, relevanten App-Zustand, Installationskennungen und begrenzte Sitzungsinformationen enthalten. Von der App erzeugte Berichte filtern ursprüngliche Fehlermeldungen und begrenzen technische Details. Wir fügen Finanzdatensätze, Namen, Geburtsdaten oder Kontaktdaten nicht absichtlich bei. Diese Filterung garantiert nicht, dass jede von Android oder einem SDK erzeugte Diagnose anonym oder frei von beiläufig enthaltenen Informationen ist.

Die App deaktiviert ihre Berichterstattung und fordert die Löschung noch nicht gesendeter Crashlytics-Berichte an. Bereits laufende Übertragungen können nicht zurückgerufen und bereits bei Google eingegangene Berichte dadurch nicht gelöscht werden. Beim erneuten Einschalten sollen ältere ausstehende Berichte verworfen werden, statt Berichte aus einem ausgeschalteten Zeitraum absichtlich hochzuladen. Der Schalter garantiert nicht, dass alle Netzwerkaktivitäten unterstützender Dienste sofort enden.

Installationskennungen sind pseudonym, nicht garantiert anonym. Weitere Informationen bieten die Datenschutzhinweise von Firebase und die Angaben zur Android-Datenverarbeitung.

Sicherheit und Grenzen der Datensicherung

Esta Política de privacidad se aplica a Financial Calculators: NalaFin, que aparece como NalaFin en su dispositivo y se publica bajo el nombre Naladimarth. Explica el tratamiento de información por la aplicación Android y esta página legal específica de la aplicación, así como sus opciones.

Los informes generados por la aplicación filtran los mensajes originales de las excepciones y limitan los detalles técnicos. La aplicación desactiva sus informes y solicita la eliminación de los informes de Crashlytics no enviados. No puede retirar una transferencia ya iniciada ni eliminar informes ya recibidos por Google. Al reactivarlos, el diseño prevé descartar informes pendientes anteriores, en vez de subir intencionadamente informes conservados durante un periodo de desactivación.
