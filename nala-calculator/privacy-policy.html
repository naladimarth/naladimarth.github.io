Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information stays on your device, what limited technical and advertising data may be processed by Google services, and the privacy choices available when using Themed Calculator.

Effective / last updated:  24 September 2026 Google Play target audience: 18+ No account required

Themed Calculator is developed and maintained by Naladimarth. Core calculator functions do not require an account. Calculation history, theme state, number-format choice, haptic preference, and walkthrough state are stored locally on the device. The app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertising, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent/privacy choices where required, and Firebase Crashlytics for stability diagnostics.

01

Overview

Themed Calculator is a calculator and theme-customization utility. Current features include calculation entry and evaluation, live result preview, cursor editing, calculation history, selectable calculator themes, Automatic (Device), International, and Indian number-format options, optional haptic feedback, and an in-app walkthrough.

The current version does not include calculator button sounds. You can use the core calculator without creating an account, signing in, or providing your name, email address, phone number, or profile information. The app does not provide cloud synchronization of calculation history.

Important: calculations entered into the calculator are processed on the device for calculator functionality. Saved calculation history remains locally on your device unless you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. The app does not intentionally upload your calculation history to Naladimarth.
02

Information stored locally on your device

The app uses local device storage to remember information needed for its features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

Calculation history

Recent calculation expressions and results. The current app stores up to 50 history entries and allows individual entries or the full history to be deleted.

Theme preferences

The selected calculator theme and the expiry time of any temporary bonus-theme unlock. A selected bonus theme may remain selected after the temporary unlock period expires.

Number-format preference

Your selected display format: Automatic (Device), International, or Indian. When Automatic (Device) is selected, the app uses device locale information on-device to select a supported display format.

Haptic preference

Whether optional haptic feedback is enabled for calculator interactions.

App-use preferences

Basic settings such as whether the introductory walkthrough has already been completed.

Legacy local preference

An older installation may still contain an unused button-sound preference written by a previous app version. The current version no longer includes button sound and does not use that legacy value.

These locally stored values are used to provide app functionality on your device. Naladimarth does not operate a user-account database for Themed Calculator and does not receive your local calculation history through an app account or cloud-sync service.

Local app data is subject to the security of your device. Other people who can access your unlocked device or device backups may also be able to access data stored by apps.

Information we do not directly request

Themed Calculator does not require you to submit account credentials, a user profile, payment-card details, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or other personal content to Naladimarth in order to use its core calculator functions.

The app does not ask you to upload calculation history to Naladimarth. However, limited technical, consent-related, and advertising-related data may be collected, shared, or processed automatically by the third-party services described below.

Advertising and bonus-theme unlocks

Themed Calculator uses optional rewarded ads. The current theme model provides 4 free themes and 12 bonus themes. A user may choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock the bonus-theme selection for 24 hours. Core calculator functionality and the free themes do not require watching an ad.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device’s general or approximate location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video/ad interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information relating to the app or advertising SDK; and
  • device or account-related identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

Depending on your region, consent status, device settings, Google settings, and ad-serving configuration, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited. Rewarded-ad availability is not guaranteed.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Android advertising-ID collection and availability are also subject to Android, Google, SDK, and user privacy controls.

07

Crash reporting and diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, group, investigate, and fix crashes and serious stability problems. Depending on the event and platform, Crashlytics may process technical information such as:

  • crash traces, stack traces, exception types, and exception messages;
  • Crashlytics installation identifiers and Firebase installation IDs;
  • session identifiers and event/crash timestamps;
  • app package name, app version, and build information;
  • device model, operating-system version, CPU architecture, memory, disk, and related technical information;
  • app foreground/background state and technical circumstances surrounding a crash; and
  • other diagnostic details made available by the operating system, runtime, or Firebase SDK.

This information is used to monitor reliability, investigate defects, prevent repeated failures, and improve app stability.

Local calculator content: Themed Calculator does not deliberately add your saved calculation history, selected theme, number-format preference, haptic preference, walkthrough state, or bonus-theme expiry as custom Crashlytics report data. Crashlytics may still receive technical exception and runtime information generated when a failure occurs.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data

Calculation history and locally stored preferences may remain on your device until you delete applicable data through the app, clear the app’s storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. The current app limits stored calculation history to the 50 most recent entries. Expired bonus-theme unlock state is removed by the app when that state is loaded and found to be expired.

A legacy button-sound preference written by an older version may remain in local app storage after updating. The current version does not use it. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data, subject to Android and device-backup behavior.

Advertising and consent data

Information processed by Google AdMob and UMP is retained according to Google’s policies, product settings, consent choices, and legal obligations.

Crashlytics data

Firebase states that Crashlytics generally keeps crash stack traces, associated installation identifiers, and extracted crash data for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems.

Because Themed Calculator does not use a user account, Naladimarth may not be able to identify a particular advertising or Crashlytics record as belonging to you based only on your name or email address.
