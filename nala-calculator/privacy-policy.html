Privacy Policy for Themed Calculator Plus

Effective date: 4 July 2026

Themed Calculator Plus is a calculator app developed and maintained by Naladimarth. The app includes features such as calculation history, theme customization, live result preview, cursor editing, and optional sound and haptic settings. This Privacy Policy explains what data may be stored or processed when you use the app.

1. Overview

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or submit personal information to use the core calculator features of the app.

The app stores some information locally on your device for app functionality and may also use third-party services for advertising and crash reporting.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To support app features, Themed Calculator Plus may store limited data locally on your device, such as:

  • calculation history
  • selected theme
  • sound and haptic preferences
  • other basic app preferences and settings

This local data is used only to provide app functionality on your device.

3. Information We Do Not Directly Request

We do not directly ask you to provide account details, profile information, contacts, photos, or similar personal content in order to use the calculator.

4. Third-Party Services

Themed Calculator Plus may use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect, receive, or process certain technical and device-related data in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party privacy information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security Information

5. Advertising

The app may display ads using Google AdMob.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and app configuration, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Ad-related services may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect or process technical data such as:

  • IP address
  • approximate location derived from IP address
  • user interaction data related to the app or ads
  • diagnostic information
  • device and advertising identifiers

This data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and related service operation.

6. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

The app may use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify, analyze, and fix crashes or stability issues.

Crash reporting services may process technical and diagnostic information such as:

  • crash reports and stack traces
  • app version information
  • device and operating system information
  • installation-related identifiers used for crash analysis

This information is used to improve app reliability, performance, and stability.

7. How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • provide calculator features and saved preferences
  • show ads and support app monetization
  • measure ad performance and help prevent fraud or abuse
  • diagnose crashes and improve app stability
  • maintain, secure, and improve the app

8. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information.

However, data may be processed by third-party service providers used in the app, such as Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics, to operate their services.

9. Data Retention

Local app data, such as calculation history and preferences, may remain on your device until you clear it from the app, clear app data through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those providers’ own policies, systems, and legal obligations.

10. Your Choices

You may be able to:

  • clear calculation history from within the app
  • reset or delete app data through your device settings
  • uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device
  • manage certain advertising or privacy controls through your device or Google settings, where available

Depending on your location, you may have certain privacy rights under applicable law. For data processed by third-party providers such as Google AdMob and Firebase, those providers may offer additional controls and information through their own privacy documentation.

11. Children’s Privacy

Themed Calculator Plus is a general audience utility app and is not intended specifically for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will review the issue and take reasonable steps as appropriate.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app and related services secure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International Processing

Third-party services used by the app may process data on servers located in different countries, depending on how those services operate.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
