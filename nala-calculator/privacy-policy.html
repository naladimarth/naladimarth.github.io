About this policy

Themed Calculator is an Android app developed by Naladimarth. Some screens call it “Nala Calculator.” This policy covers the app, the support email you can choose to send, and visits to this policy page.

This policy describes Themed Calculator 2.0.0 for Android. If you use an earlier release, the features available to you, the ads you see and the privacy controls you can use may differ.

For information about calculation accuracy, rounding and the limits of relying on results, see the Themed Calculator Terms and Conditions.

What the app remembers on your device

The calculator evaluates expressions on your device. You can use basic and scientific calculations, unit conversions and offline tools without creating an account. Naladimarth does not operate a server that receives or synchronizes your calculator records.

Calculations and memory
Expressions, displayed results, stored numeric values, recent answers and calculator memory let you recall and reuse a calculation. If you enable Restore unfinished input, the app can reopen a draft when you return.
History and notebooks
Saved entries can include a timestamp, calculator mode, expression, result, favorite status, notes, folder and tags. Notebooks and saved formulas can contain text you enter yourself. A label such as a customer’s name becomes part of the saved record if you type it there.
Tools and appearance
The app can remember tool inputs, graph expressions and ranges, favorites, theme drafts, the active theme, and display choices such as number separators, precision and text size. Where device-based formatting is selected, the app can use the device locale to format a number locally.
Passes and preferences
The app keeps local records of earned theme access, timing and expiry, along with preferences such as crash reporting and whether you have seen the guide. The calculator does not ask for contact details to assign a theme pass.

Home-screen widget. The Android widget can keep and display its own expression and result. A person who can see your home screen may see the value shown there.

What a private session does

Turn on Controls → Behaviour → Private session to do a calculation without adding it to your ordinary history. The calculator sets aside your ordinary input and starts a temporary workspace. Its temporary memory begins with your current calculator memory, so an existing value may be available when you recall it. Changes made to that memory during the session are temporary.

When you leave, the app discards the temporary calculator history and memory changes and restores the ordinary session. Temporary calculator history and temporary memory changes are excluded from the app’s JSON backup. If you explicitly save a formula, edit a notebook or save a theme, that item goes into ordinary app storage and can appear in a later backup. You can still explicitly copy a value or share it. Something you put on the Android clipboard, send to a messaging app, or save as a separate file can remain there after the session ends.

Private session controls calculator storage. Banner ads, regional ad-consent checks and your separate crash-reporting preference still apply.

Choosing a photo for a theme

When you choose a background photo, Android opens its photo or document picker. You grant the app access to the selected item. The app reads it, adjusts its orientation when needed, resizes large images and stores a local copy for the theme. This feature does not request camera access or permission to browse your entire photo library.

You can change or remove the photo in the theme editor. If you are editing a saved theme, save the change to update that theme. The original photo in your gallery and copies already embedded in other saved themes, shared files or backups are separate.

The app does not upload theme photos to Naladimarth. If you choose an image from a cloud-backed provider in Android’s picker, that provider may retrieve it for you under its own privacy policy.

What goes into a shared file

You decide when to copy, save or share. Android handles the clipboard, file destination and share sheet. A drive or messaging service you choose may upload the content to its servers, and a recipient may keep another copy. Here is what the app places in each output.

Contents by export type
Your choiceWhat it can contain
Copy or pasteThe value you choose to copy, such as the expression, displayed answer or stored value. Paste reads text from the system clipboard when you invoke it.
History textThe date, mode, expression, displayed result and note for each selected entry. Copying or sharing history text can therefore include a note.
History CSVThe record ID, mode, date, expression, stored result, note, folder and tags. The stored result can contain more precision than the displayed answer.
Image or receiptA calculation image shows selected expressions and results. A receipt also prints the saved date and note. Sharing a graph PNG sends an image of the graph.
Notebook, formula or constantSharing a notebook sends its title and lines, including notes, named expressions and calculated results. Sharing a formula result sends its name, answer and unit. Sharing a built-in constant sends its name, value, unit and source.
Theme fileThe theme name and design settings. Include background photo starts off. Turning it on embeds the picture in the file so its recipient can access it.

For file sharing, the app can create a temporary copy in its cache and grant the receiving app read access. When another file is shared, the app attempts to remove older cached share files. Clearing the sender’s cache does not delete a recipient’s copy.

Calculator backups and Android backups

A file you create in the app

Settings → Local backup and restore lets you save a readable JSON file to a destination you select. It can contain histories and notes, saved formulas and notebooks, calculator memory and last answer, settings, favorites, drafts, graph data and saved or draft themes with their photos. Earned passes are excluded from the transferable file.

A theme export and a full backup handle photos differently. Leaving the photo switch off when you share one theme protects that export. The photo can still be present in a full calculator backup. The app does not add a password or an encryption layer to the JSON file.

When restoring, you choose the file and review its contents. Merge adds eligible saved records that are missing and keeps current preferences. Replace applies the backed-up data, which can include preferences. The app saves a local recovery snapshot before applying a restore and attempts to delete it once the restore succeeds. If the restore or cleanup is interrupted, a snapshot can remain and the app may offer to restore it. Review your crash-reporting preference after replacing settings.

If the app detects damaged storage, Export original recovery files can open the Android share sheet with a ZIP of original database files and available recovery files. This archive can expose the underlying calculator records and preferences, including data outside the ordinary JSON backup. Choose its recipient with care.

Backup or transfer performed by Android

Separately, Android or the device manufacturer may include eligible app files and preferences in a system backup or a phone-to-phone transfer. This depends on the device, operating system, backup provider and your settings. Reinstalling or setting up a replacement phone can restore data from such a backup. To manage those copies, use the device or backup provider’s controls.

When Google receives advertising data

The app uses Google AdMob for banner ads displayed during ordinary use and rewarded ads for optional theme features. The app shows a notice before requesting a rewarded ad. Declining a reward lets you continue calculating. A banner can still request an ad.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share the following information with Google and, as applicable, advertising partners:

  • Network information. An IP address, which can indicate the general area of a device. The calculator does not request GPS permission.
  • Device identifiers. Identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID, depending on the device, settings and SDK.
  • Interactions. Events such as an app launch, an ad impression, a tap or a video view, used to deliver and measure advertising.
  • Technical diagnostics. Performance information, such as startup time or app hangs, used for measurement, service reliability and fraud prevention.

The app does not add your saved expressions, notes, memory values or background photos to ad requests. Naladimarth does not sell or rent calculator records. Google and participating advertising partners can process advertising data for delivery, personalization where permitted, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it sends in transit.

Your advertising choices

Google’s User Messaging Platform checks the applicable consent status, including at app startup. Where required, it presents a message before an ad request. Depending on your region, choices and provider settings, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. Consent choices and related signals may be stored and communicated to providers so they can apply your selection.

Open Settings → Privacy choices when Google makes that option available. You may also manage the advertising ID in Android settings where supported. Turning off crash reporting controls a separate service. Advertising and consent data are retained according to the providers’ policies, your choices and applicable requirements.

Optional crash and stability reports

Crash reporting is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings → Crash reporting. Naladimarth uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to understand where software fails, how many installations are affected and which fixes to prioritize.

A report can include the type of error, time of the event, a technical trace of the code running at the time, app version and build, device model, operating system, app state, and session or installation identifiers. These identifiers help group reports across launches. The diagnostic messages the app writes exclude calculator expressions, history, notebook contents and photos.

Once you save the off preference, the app stops forwarding its own diagnostic messages. On Android, Firebase applies the change to automatic crash reporting on the next run of the app. While automatic reporting is off, the SDK can keep unsent reports on the device. If you enable reporting again, pending reports may be sent. Turning reporting off does not withdraw reports already delivered. Reset app data restores the default, so turn reporting off again after a reset if you prefer.

Google says Crashlytics keeps stack traces, extracted crash data and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems.

Where to delete each copy

Calculator records have no account-based expiry date. In version 2.0.0, ordinary history accepts up to 50 saved entries per supported mode. Once a mode is full, a new result is not saved to that history until you make room. Existing records above the limit can remain after an upgrade or restore.

One calculation or saved item
Delete the record in History or its corresponding saved-items screen. If you previously exported it, remove the exported file separately.
Active calculator data
Settings → Reset app data clears active history, memory, answers, formulas, notebooks, drafts, themes and their photos, then restores default preferences. Earned passes, platform-managed consent and original damaged recovery files can remain. A local pre-restore snapshot can also remain.
All private app files on this device
Use Android’s app settings to clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it. Clearing only the cache leaves saved calculator records in place. Device backups, exported files, emailed copies and originals in your gallery have their own locations.
Information already sent
Delete an external backup through the storage provider you selected. For material sent to another person, contact the recipient. For support correspondence held by Naladimarth, use the privacy contact below. Clearing your phone cannot withdraw a delivered message or Google report.

Device permissions and safeguards

The app uses Android app-private storage for its working data. The release includes internet and network-state access for online services and advertising-related permissions used by Google’s SDK. Calculating, choosing a photo and importing a backup do not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera, precise location or entire photo library.

For files you select, Android grants access to the chosen item or destination. When you share a file, the app supplies a read grant to the receiving application. A person who can unlock your phone, access the destination account or open an exported JSON or CSV file may be able to read the contents. Choose a private destination for records that contain personal notes or photos.

Email, external links and this website

Contact support, Suggest a feature and Report a bug open a draft in your email app. For a bug report, the app fills in prompts, the app version and the language label. Calculations and history are not automatically attached. You can edit, discard or send the draft.

If you send it, Naladimarth and its email provider receive the sender address, message, attachments and ordinary email metadata. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and follow up. Correspondence is retained as needed for those purposes or applicable obligations. Please omit private calculations and identifying details from screenshots unless they are needed for your request.

Links to our website, Google Play and provider policies open an external destination. The destination receives an ordinary web request and applies its own privacy practices. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. The page itself uses no analytics scripts, external fonts or advertising tags.

Your privacy rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Naladimarth, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where it applies, or contact your local privacy authority. Use the advertising privacy form for the choices it offers, and the crash-reporting switch for diagnostics.

To make a request, tell us which feature or correspondence it concerns and what you would like us to do. For example, if you want a support email deleted, its subject and approximate date can help us find it. We may ask for enough information to confirm that the correspondence is yours. Please do not send identity documents, passwords or a full calculator backup with an initial inquiry.

Because the app has no calculator account, we cannot look up the history or photos on your phone by email address. An email address alone may also be insufficient to identify a Google advertising or crash record. We can explain the controls available for the information you describe.
