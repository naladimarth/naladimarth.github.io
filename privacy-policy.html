1. Core App Use and Local Data

The app does not require an account, login, registration, or cloud account operated by Naladimarth.

The core screen-light, colour-control, fullscreen, strobe, lock, and Pixel Check features are designed to operate locally on your device and do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, files, photos, or other personal content.

Certain app preferences and privacy choices may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your settings and choices between sessions. This can include settings such as your optional usage-analytics preference.

Naladimarth does not operate a user-account database or cloud-storage backend for the app.

Network access may still be used by third-party services described in this Policy, including Firebase Crashlytics, optional Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), external links, and email functionality.

2. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to help identify crashes, diagnose technical problems, and improve app stability.

Depending on the crash or diagnostic event, Firebase Crashlytics may process technical information such as:

  • Crashlytics installation identifiers and Firebase installation identifiers
  • Crash traces, exception information, and diagnostic logs
  • App package, version, and build information
  • Device model and CPU architecture
  • Android operating system version
  • Device memory, storage, and other technical state relevant to a crash
  • Whether the app was in the foreground or background when a crash occurred
  • Timestamps and other technical information needed to diagnose a failure

Naladimarth uses this information for app stability, troubleshooting, compatibility, and quality improvement.

The app does not intentionally attach your name, email address, phone number, contacts, files, advertising ID, or other user-entered personal content to Crashlytics reports.

Crash and diagnostic information processed by Firebase is subject to Google/Firebase privacy, security, and retention practices.

3. Optional Usage Analytics

Usage Analytics is optional and OFF by default. Analytics collection is enabled only when you choose to enable “Share usage analytics” in the app.

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase to help Naladimarth understand aggregate app usage and improve the product.

You can later turn usage analytics off again from the app's privacy/settings controls.

When usage analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics may process standard Analytics information and limited, coarse app-interaction events, such as:

  • App/session and engagement information
  • App version and device/operating-system information
  • General feature or mode usage
  • Menu, guide, feedback, or control actions represented by bounded event categories
  • Aggregate geographic information provided by the Analytics service

The app's Analytics implementation is designed not to log:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • User-entered free text
  • Device serial numbers
  • Android Advertising ID for Firebase Analytics
  • Raw advertising-consent strings
  • Exact RGB, hue, brightness, or speed values from normal control interactions
  • Files, photos, contacts, microphone recordings, or camera content

Firebase Analytics Advertising ID collection is disabled for Analytics in the app's Android configuration.

Analytics ad-personalisation signals are also disabled by default for this Analytics implementation.

Turning usage analytics off stops future Analytics collection by the app on that installation. It may not automatically remove information that was already processed or aggregated by Google before the setting was disabled.

Advertising consent and Usage Analytics are separate choices. A choice made in Google's advertising consent form does not automatically enable the app's optional Firebase Usage Analytics setting.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The app may use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertising in versions where advertising is enabled.

The ad-enabled experience is designed to use one anchored adaptive banner on the normal Home control screen.

The app is designed not to display the banner in clean functional modes such as:

  • Fullscreen colour display
  • Pixel Check
  • Strobe mode
  • Locked clean-display mode

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Depending on the device, region, user choices, and applicable law, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location
  • Ad and app interaction information, such as ad views, taps, and related product interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information relating to the app and advertising SDK
  • Device or app identifiers, which may include the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, or other permitted identifiers

Advertising information may be processed by Google and eligible advertising partners in accordance with your privacy choices, applicable law, Google's policies, and the advertising configuration used by the app.

Ads may be personalised, non-personalised, or otherwise limited depending on the user's consent choices, region, device settings, applicable law, and Google's advertising systems.

Android users can reset or delete the Advertising ID through supported Android privacy settings. Availability and behaviour depend on the Android version and device.

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related privacy choices where required.

The app may request an updated advertising-consent status when it starts.

This does not mean a consent message is shown every time the app opens.

Google UMP determines whether a privacy or consent message is required based on factors such as applicable regulation, region, the current consent state, and the Privacy & messaging configuration associated with the app.

For example, European-regulations consent requirements may apply to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Other privacy messages may be used where applicable.

If a consent decision is required, UMP may present options such as consenting, declining consent, or managing detailed privacy choices.

When an advertising consent requirement applies, the app is designed not to request advertising until UMP indicates that advertising requests are permitted.

Where Google UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the app will provide a way for users to reopen and change relevant advertising privacy choices.

Advertising consent handled through UMP remains separate from the optional Firebase Usage Analytics setting described in Section 3.

6. Feedback – Report a Bug and Request a Feature

The app provides Report a bug and Request a feature actions using your device's email application.

Naladimarth does not use a separate feedback backend for these actions.

Before reading optional device details for a feedback email, the app asks whether you want to continue and include those details.

If you choose to continue, the app may automatically prepare:

  • App name
  • App version
  • App build number
  • Android version
  • Device manufacturer
  • Device model
  • Device locale

The app does not automatically add:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • Device serial number
  • Files or photos
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Crash logs
  • Other user content

The email is opened in your email application for review. You decide whether to send it and may edit or remove its contents before sending.

If you send an email to Naladimarth, the email address, message, and any information you voluntarily add are transmitted through your email provider and received by Naladimarth for support or feedback purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app preferences remain on your device until they are changed, app data is cleared, or the app is removed, subject to Android device backup and restore behaviour.

Naladimarth does not maintain a user account or server-side profile for the core app.

Data processed through Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, and UMP is retained and handled according to the applicable Google service settings, policies, and legal requirements.

Because these services generally use technical or pseudonymous identifiers rather than an account managed by Naladimarth, Naladimarth may not always be able to identify or retrieve information associated with a particular individual device from a general deletion request.

Turning off optional Usage Analytics stops future Analytics collection for that app installation, but does not necessarily delete previously processed or aggregated Analytics information.

Where advertising privacy options are required, users may use the in-app privacy-options entry point to review or change applicable advertising choices.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, resolve issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit the information handled directly by the app.

Google states that information transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Firebase services also use Google security infrastructure for data transmission and processing.

No method of electronic transmission, processing, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Emails you choose to send are also subject to the security and privacy practices of your email provider.

9. Permissions and Device Access

The app uses device capabilities required for its screen-light, brightness, display-mode, keep-screen-awake, and related functionality.

The app does not request access to sensitive device content such as:

  • Precise or background location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos, videos, or general file storage
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs

Third-party services such as Firebase and Google AdMob use internet/network access to provide their services.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may declare Android's Advertising ID permission so that the SDK can access the Advertising ID where available and legally permitted.

This is not a runtime permission that gives the app access to contacts, files, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

Firebase Analytics Advertising ID collection is separately disabled in the app's Analytics configuration.
