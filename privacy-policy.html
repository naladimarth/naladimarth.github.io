1. Core App Use and Local Data

The app does not require an account, login, registration, subscription account, or cloud account operated by Naladimarth.

Core features such as the Preview Box, Hue Wheel, Shade control, Strobe, Auto Color, Brightness control, Pixel Check, White, Full Screen, Fluid Colors, and Lock are designed to operate locally on your device.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, camera content, microphone recordings, or precise location in order to use the app's core features.

Certain preferences and choices may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember them between sessions. These may include app settings, brightness-related preferences, first-use or guide state, safety acknowledgements, and your optional Usage Analytics choice.

Naladimarth does not operate a user-account database or a cloud-storage backend for the app's core functionality.

Network access is used by third-party services described in this Policy, including Firebase Crashlytics, optional Google Analytics for Firebase, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), external links, and email functionality.

2. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to help identify crashes and serious technical failures, improve app stability, and diagnose compatibility problems.

Crashlytics is separate from the optional Usage Analytics setting. Turning Usage Analytics off does not turn Crashlytics off.

Firebase Crashlytics may automatically process technical and diagnostic information associated with a crash or error, including:

  • Crash stack traces and exception information
  • Relevant application state at the time of a crash
  • App package, version, and build information
  • Android operating-system information
  • Device model, CPU architecture, memory, and storage information
  • Foreground/background state and crash timestamps
  • Crashlytics installation identifiers
  • Firebase installation and session identifiers used by Firebase services

The app does not set a user account identifier in Crashlytics and does not intentionally attach your name, email address, phone number, contacts, files, Advertising ID, or user-entered support text to Crashlytics reports.

Crash and diagnostic information is handled under Google's Firebase terms, security measures, and retention practices.

3. Optional Usage Analytics

Usage Analytics is optional and OFF by default. There is no first-run Analytics consent popup. Analytics is enabled only if you open Improve Experience in the app and choose to enable Share usage analytics.

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase to help Naladimarth understand aggregate feature usage and improve the app.

When Usage Analytics is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase may process standard automatically collected Analytics information such as app/session engagement, app and operating-system information, device characteristics, approximate geographic information, automatically collected events/properties, and app-installation or Analytics identifiers.

The app also sends a limited set of custom event categories when Analytics is enabled. These indicate which type of control, mode, guide, menu item, or feedback action was used. Examples include:

  • Control categories such as Hue Wheel, Shade, Brightness, Strobe speed, and Auto Color speed
  • Mode categories such as Full Screen, White, Fluid Colors, Lock, Pixel Check, Strobe, and Auto Color
  • Quick Guide and How to Use actions such as opened, completed, or skipped
  • Menu categories such as Report a bug, Request a feature, Rate us, More apps, Privacy Policy, Terms, and About
  • Feedback-flow categories such as disclosure shown, accepted, cancelled, email composer opened, or composer failed

The app's custom Analytics implementation is designed not to send:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • User-entered free text
  • Device serial numbers
  • Raw advertising-consent strings
  • Exact RGB, hue, brightness, strobe-speed, or Auto Color values from normal control interactions
  • Files, photos, contacts, camera content, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location

Firebase Analytics Advertising ID collection is disabled in the app's Android Analytics configuration.

Analytics ad-personalisation signals are also disabled by default in the app's Analytics configuration.

You can turn Usage Analytics off again at any time from Improve Experience. Turning it off stops future Analytics collection by the app on that installation. It does not necessarily erase information that was already processed or aggregated before the setting was disabled.

Advertising privacy choices and Usage Analytics are separate. A choice made in Google's advertising consent or privacy message does not automatically enable the app's optional Usage Analytics setting.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to support advertising.

The app is designed to show at most one anchored adaptive banner on the normal Home control screen when the Home screen is eligible to display an ad.

The banner is designed not to appear in functional clean-display modes or modal/privacy flows, including:

  • Pixel Check
  • White
  • Full Screen colour display
  • Fluid Colors
  • Strobe mode
  • Locked clean-display mode
  • Quick Guide and How to Use
  • Feedback flows
  • Improve Experience / Usage Analytics settings
  • UMP consent or Ad privacy choices screens

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain technical information for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes. Depending on the device, region, privacy choices, and applicable law, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location
  • User and ad interaction information such as app launches, ad views, taps, and related product interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information such as app/SDK performance, launch time, hangs, or energy use
  • Device or app identifiers, which may include the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, or other permitted identifiers

The app does not request precise GPS location for advertising. General location may still be inferred by Google from information such as an IP address.

Ads may be personalised, non-personalised, restricted, or limited depending on applicable law, your privacy choices, device settings, Google's advertising systems, and the configuration of the advertising services.

Android users can reset or delete the Advertising ID through supported Android privacy controls. Availability and behaviour depend on the Android version and device.

U.S. state privacy messages

For eligible users in U.S. states with applicable privacy laws, Google's U.S. state regulations message may provide privacy choices relating to the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, as those terms are defined by applicable law.

Advertising choices handled through UMP remain separate from the optional Usage Analytics setting described in Section 3.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app preferences generally remain on your device until they are changed, app data is cleared, or the app is removed, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour.

Naladimarth does not maintain a server-side user profile for the app's core functionality.

Firebase Analytics information, when you have enabled Usage Analytics, is retained according to the applicable Google/Firebase Analytics property settings, product configuration, policies, and legal requirements.

Advertising and consent information processed by Google AdMob and UMP is retained according to Google's applicable service settings, policies, consent records, and legal requirements.

Turning Usage Analytics off stops future Analytics collection by the app on that installation but does not necessarily delete information that was already processed or aggregated.

Where Ad privacy choices are available, you can use them to review or change applicable advertising choices. Android Advertising ID controls may also be used where supported.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, troubleshoot the reported issue, maintain basic support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations.

You may contact Naladimarth to request deletion of support emails or other information that Naladimarth directly controls and can reasonably identify from your request. Contact support@naladimarth.com .

This request mechanism does not mean that Naladimarth can identify, retrieve, or directly delete all information processed by Google AdMob, UMP, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics. Some Google-managed information is associated with pseudonymous installation, advertising, or service identifiers and is subject to Google's own controls, retention practices, contractual terms, and applicable law.

The app does not request runtime access to sensitive device content such as:

  • Precise or background location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos, videos, or general file storage
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs

Internet/network access is used by Firebase, Google AdMob, UMP, external links, and email-related actions.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add Android's Advertising ID permission to the final app manifest so the advertising SDK can access the Android Advertising ID where it is available, legally permitted, and not restricted by device or privacy settings.

Firebase Analytics Advertising ID collection is separately disabled in the app's Analytics configuration. That Analytics setting does not prevent the Google Mobile Ads SDK from using the Advertising ID for advertising where allowed.
