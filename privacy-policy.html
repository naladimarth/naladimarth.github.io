Privacy Policy for RGB Screen & Pixel Check

Last updated: January 2026

RGB Screen & Pixel Check (also known as “RGB Screen Light · Pixel Check”, “the app”) is developed and published by Naladimarth. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Data Collection & Usage

The app does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.

The app works fully offline for its core features and does not require user accounts, logins, or cloud storage.

Limited technical data may be collected for crash reporting purposes, as described below.

2. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash reporting service provided by Google, to monitor app stability and diagnose technical issues.

Crashlytics may collect the following technical information:

  • Crashlytics Installation UUID
  • Device model and CPU architecture
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This information is used solely to improve app stability and performance and does not directly identify individual users.

All data transmitted for crash reporting is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

Learn more about Google’s privacy practices here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Retention & Deletion

The app does not maintain user accounts and does not store personal data on its own servers.

Technical crash and diagnostic data collected by Firebase Crashlytics is retained according to Google’s standard data retention policies and is automatically deleted after a limited period.

If you would like to request deletion of any crash or diagnostic data associated with your app installation, you may contact us using the details below. We will assist you in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect any technical data processed by the app. All crash and diagnostic data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit using industry-standard cryptographic protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child has provided any information through the app, please contact us and appropriate action will be taken.

6. Permissions

The app only uses system permissions required for its core screen display and testing functionality.

The app does not request or access sensitive permissions such as:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Files or media storage

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws such as the GDPR (European Union) or CCPA (California).

Legal Basis: The app processes limited technical crash and diagnostic data based on legitimate interest in maintaining app stability, security, and performance. The app does not collect names, email addresses, or other direct personal identifiers.

If you have questions regarding your rights, please contact us using the details below.
