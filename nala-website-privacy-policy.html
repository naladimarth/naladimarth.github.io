Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

You can explore our apps and web tools without creating an account. You choose whether to share website analytics. If you email us, we use the details you send to respond.

This policy covers the Naladimarth landing website. The Finance Calculators website and our Android apps explain their own data practices separately.

When you visit

Your browser requests the page, images and scripts from our host. GitHub Pages records the IP address used for that connection for security. This happens when you visit, regardless of your analytics choice. GitHub Pages data collection

The water-silk animation responds to pointer movement in your browser. The animation uses the pointer position to draw the effect locally. You can pause it in the footer.

Your analytics choice

On the live website, we use Google Analytics 4 only with your permission. It helps us understand which pages people visit, how they find the site and which product links they use. Depending on the event, Google may receive the page address, referral source, browser and device details, approximate location, and a cookie identifier that helps distinguish visits. We keep advertising personalisation and Google signals disabled.

Open Privacy settings in the footer to allow analytics or turn it off. The site saves your selection in your browser’s local storage so it can apply your choice on later visits from that browser. Clearing site data removes the saved choice. A different browser or domain has its own choice.

Turning analytics off stops future measurement on this site. To ask about information already collected, email us using the address below. How Google handles data from sites that use its services

When you email us

The contact button opens your email app. If you send a message, we and our email provider receive your email address, the message, any attachments and the sender name your email app includes. We use these details to answer questions, investigate issues, consider suggestions or discuss a collaboration.

Product links

App download buttons open Google Play. The finance button opens the separate Finance Calculators website. When you follow a link, the destination receives a request from your browser and its own privacy notice applies. For RGB Screen & Pixel Check, Themed Calculator and NalaFin, you can find each app’s privacy policy in its Google Play listing.

Retention and processing

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the conversation, follow up on an issue or meet a legal obligation. Google Analytics retains user and event data according to our property’s retention setting. Aggregate reports can remain available after individual event data expires. Hosting, analytics and email providers may process data outside your country. Google Analytics retention settings

Privacy questions and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain uses, or to contact your local data protection authority.

For a request about an email conversation, write from the address you used and mention the subject or approximate date. Website analytics generally use browser identifiers, so an email address alone may be insufficient to locate a visit.
