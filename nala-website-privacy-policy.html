Website policy only. This page applies to the Naladimarth website. Naladimarth apps have separate privacy policies that describe how each app handles information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the public website available at https://naladimarth.com and pages that expressly link to this policy. It does not replace the separate privacy policies for Naladimarth mobile applications or other products.

2. Information handled when you visit the website

You can browse the Naladimarth website without creating an account or providing your name, phone number, or other account information. The website currently does not provide user login or account features.

Technical information may nevertheless be processed when the site is delivered to your browser. Website hosting, security, and infrastructure providers may process standard request information needed to provide and protect the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested page, and request date or time.

3. Google Analytics

Naladimarth may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors discover and use the website and to improve website performance, content, and usability.

Depending on your interaction with the site and the Analytics settings in use, Analytics information may include:

  • pages viewed and navigation activity;
  • session and engagement information;
  • approximate geographic information;
  • browser, device type, operating system, and language information;
  • referral source or traffic channel;
  • interactions such as scrolling, outbound link clicks, file downloads, site-search interactions, video engagement, or form interactions where those features are present and measurement is enabled; and
  • cookies or similar pseudonymous identifiers where permitted and enabled.

Naladimarth does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other information that directly identifies a visitor to Google Analytics.

Google states that Google Analytics uses identifiers such as cookies to measure interactions and uses IP addresses for service operation, security, and approximate location. Google also states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses.

Learn more in Google’s Analytics data safeguards information and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Cookies, similar technologies, and consent

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish browsers or devices and measure website activity. Analytics cookies are not required for the basic operation of the Naladimarth website.

Naladimarth currently requires an affirmative choice before Google Analytics is enabled on this website. Until you choose to accept analytics, the Google Analytics tag remains blocked. If you reject analytics, Google Analytics remains disabled.

The website stores your analytics preference in your browser's local storage so that it can remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics. This first-party preference is used only to apply your privacy choice and is not sent to Google Analytics.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Google provides a Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on for supported browsers.

5. Why we use analytics information

When Analytics is enabled, Naladimarth uses the resulting information for purposes such as:

  • understanding how visitors reach the website;
  • measuring page usage and engagement;
  • identifying technical or usability problems;
  • understanding which content and product links are useful;
  • improving site structure, performance, and design; and
  • producing aggregated statistics about website usage.

6. Legal basis and privacy choices

Privacy laws differ by jurisdiction. Where consent is legally required for non-essential Analytics cookies or related processing, the relevant processing will be based on your consent. In locations where another lawful basis is available, processing may be based on Naladimarth’s legitimate interest in understanding website performance and improving its products and website, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

You may withdraw or change your analytics choice at any time by selecting Privacy settings in the website footer. You can also use browser controls or Google’s opt-out mechanism described above.

7. Service providers and data processing

Naladimarth relies on third-party service providers to operate parts of the website. These providers may process information only as necessary for the services they provide, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Google Analytics

Google processes Analytics data to provide the Google Analytics measurement service. Google’s handling of information is governed by its applicable terms, data-processing terms, and privacy policies.

Website hosting and infrastructure

Hosting, domain, security, and network providers may process technical information necessary to deliver the website, maintain availability, prevent abuse, and protect their services.

8. Data retention

Analytics information is retained according to the Google Analytics property settings selected by Naladimarth and Google’s applicable retention rules. Naladimarth may also retain aggregated or non-identifying reports for business analysis where permitted.

We aim to retain website information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, security, and dispute resolution.

9. International processing

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate contractual or legal safeguards may apply to such transfers under the service provider’s terms and applicable law.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal data, which can include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with an applicable data-protection authority.

Because website analytics data is generally pseudonymous or aggregated and the website does not require visitor accounts, Naladimarth may not always be able to identify or isolate information relating to a particular visitor from an Analytics request alone. We will respond to valid privacy requests as required by applicable law and based on the information reasonably available to us.

11. Children’s privacy

The Naladimarth website is a general-audience product and company website and is not specifically directed to children. Naladimarth does not knowingly ask children to provide names, contact details, or account information through this website.

12. Security

13. External links

The website contains links to third-party services, including app stores and other external websites. When you follow an external link, that third party’s privacy policy and terms apply to its collection and use of information. Naladimarth does not control third-party privacy practices.

14. Changes to this policy

Naladimarth may update this Privacy Policy when the website, analytics configuration, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
